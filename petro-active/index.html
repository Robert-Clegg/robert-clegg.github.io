MISSION BRIEFING

Welcome to Arctic Research Station Alpha. Your team has been deployed to this sector to locate subsurface hydrocarbon reserves.

Geological surveys indicate sedimentary basins formed 120–150 million years ago lie beneath this terrain. Ancient organic matter — plankton and marine organisms — was buried under layers of sediment, compressed and heated over millions of years into kerogen, and eventually matured into crude oil and natural gas.

Your objective: Deploy a network of geophones across the survey grid, trigger seismic sources to send energy waves into the ground, then interpret the reflected signals on your oscilloscope to identify the most promising drill site.

The rock layers underground will reflect seismic waves differently depending on their density and composition. Watch for changes in amplitude and frequency — low-frequency shadows may indicate oil or gas pockets trapped beneath cap rock.
